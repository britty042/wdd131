Overview

Purpose

The purpose of this website is to build an online presence that invites a broader clientelle for our laser cutting design business. We would love for people to come and invest in our product, and to learn a little about laser cutting, thus resulting in a greater profit margin for Dreammaker Design.

Audience

Our target audience for this website includes decorators and crafters. We hope to target the middle to upper class, ranging in age from early 20's to 80's, with interests in finely made, laser cut wood crafting. We seek to fill the need for high-quality products at reasonable prices.

Dynamic elements

I plan to use javascript to add a dark mode option to my site, and to open a minimized menu on the home page in mobile view. I also plan to use javascript to display a message after "add to cart" is clicked. Lastly, I will use javascript to display a random items we sell on the home page.

Shop

This section will include shopping/ordering of laser cut items, generally unfinished for wood crafting. Each image will have a description and an "Add to Cart" button. People will come to my website primarily to shop for décor or wood crafting products including as well as unfinished products for crafting, so we would definitely need a “shop” section. Visitors will primarily want to shop on our website. To make this page successful, I will definitely need pricing information for the products that we will sell. This information can be gathered based on current pricing of supplies (wood, paint and finishes, etc.), and would be posted on the site as a set price, no negotiations. Most of this information would be readily available at my local wood supply store, or available using their online website as well. I would also need a little information as to what is “trending” right now in the wood crafting/laser cut sector. I can gather this information via other websites, and simply by watching and reviewing the designs sold at local craft stores near me and around my area. In doing so, I would ensure that items we make would actually sell.
